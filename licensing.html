Beat Licensing Guide

Each beat license should state exactly what you may do with the beat, what files you receive, any stream or distribution limits, and whether royalties or publishing obligations apply.

Lease

A non-exclusive license. The producer may license the same beat to other artists. Check the individual license for file formats, usage limits and any royalty split.

Premium Lease

A non-exclusive license that may include additional files or broader usage rights. The exact benefits and any royalty obligations must be written in the specific license.

Exclusive Rights

An exclusive purchase should specify which rights transfer, which rights remain with the producer, whether prior licenses remain valid, and how the beat is removed from future marketplace sales. “Exclusive” does not automatically mean every copyright or publishing right transfers.

Before checkout: the written license and checkout summary are the terms to rely on. If the royalty split, ownership transfer or permitted use is unclear, do not purchase until the team clarifies it.
