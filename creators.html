QUESTIONS

Before You Dive In

Do I need to pay to see producer profiles?

No. Every profile — bio, socials, and full beat catalog — is free to browse for anyone, signed in or not. You only pay when you actually license a specific beat.

Do I need an account just to look around?

No account needed to browse. You'll only be asked to sign up when you want to follow a producer, message one, or check out with a beat.

How does following a producer work?

Tap Follow on their card or profile. If you're not signed in yet, we'll prompt you to create a free account first — following itself never costs anything.

How do I become a featured creator?

Apply as a producer. LyTune hasn't launched its producer roster yet, so early applicants get founding-producer visibility with far less competition than later arrivals.

Why don't I see any producers yet?

LyTune is pre-launch for producers — nobody's created a profile yet. This page is live and ready; it's just waiting for its first creators.
