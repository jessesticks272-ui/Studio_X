Privacy Notice

This notice describes the main categories of information a music marketplace may process. It must be checked against the live Firebase, analytics, payment and hosting configuration before being treated as a complete legal privacy notice.

Information you provide

Account details such as your name, email address, profile information, messages, and creator uploads may be processed to operate platform features.

Technical information

Hosting and security systems may process technical information such as browser details, device information, log events and approximate network information to keep the service working and protect accounts.

Payments

Payment information should be handled by the selected payment provider. Do not send card numbers or passwords to LyTune support. The final notice should identify the payment providers actually used.

Use and sharing

Information may be used to provide requested features, maintain security, respond to support requests and meet legal obligations. The final notice should name the actual service providers and explain retention, access, deletion and user-rights procedures.

Questions or requests

Use the contact page to ask a privacy question or request help with account information.
