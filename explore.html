QUESTIONS

Before You Buy

Do I need an account to browse or preview beats?

No — filtering, searching, and previewing are all free with no account. You'll only be asked to sign up at checkout.

What's the difference between the license types?

Lease is the affordable entry point (capped streams, producer keeps most royalties). Premium Lease adds stems and a higher cap. Exclusive Rights transfers full ownership and removes the beat from sale entirely. See "How Licensing Works" above for details.

Can I get a refund after buying a license?

Digital license policies vary by producer and will be shown at checkout before you pay — nothing is charged without you seeing the terms first.

Why is the grid empty right now?

LyTune is pre-launch for producers — nobody's uploaded a beat yet. The filters, search, and licensing all work exactly the same once real beats start coming in.

I'm a producer — how do I get my beats listed here?

Apply as a founding producer. Early producers get listed with far less competition than producers who join after launch.
