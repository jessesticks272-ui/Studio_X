QUESTIONS

About Trending

How often does the trending list update?

It's calculated from real, recent activity (plays, sales, likes), so it updates as that activity happens — not on a fixed schedule and never manually curated.

Can a producer pay to trend?

No. There's no way to buy placement here — trending is entirely driven by real listener and buyer activity.

Why is everything empty right now?

LyTune hasn't launched to producers yet, so there's no real activity to rank. The mechanics are fully built and ready — this page will populate the moment real plays and sales start happening.

How do I get my beats to trend?

Apply as a founding producer, then drive plays and sales however you normally promote your work — shares, socials, your existing fanbase.
