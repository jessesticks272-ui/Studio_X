QUESTIONS

About the Market

What's the difference between Market and Explore?

Explore is specifically for browsing and filtering beats. Market is the wider hub — beats plus stems, sound kits, presets, full song placements, and services, all in one place.

When will stems, kits, and services be available?

Those categories open once the first producers start uploading to them. Tap "Get Notified" on any category and we'll reach out when it goes live.

Can I offer mixing or mastering as a producer?

That's the plan for the Mixing & Mastering category. Apply now and you'll be part of the first wave once it opens.

How do bundles work?

A bundle groups multiple items — a beat with its stems, a themed pack, a producer's full kit — at one combined price. Any producer will be able to build one once the feature opens.
